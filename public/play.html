Meadow

Experimental version of Meadow directly in your web browser, running with Ruffle. Expect things to be unstable at the current stage.

Offline
The game is using a lot of memory, and the browser may close it soon. A restart frees it.

Play full screen

Safari on an iPhone keeps its bars around a page. To play without them, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Meadow from your Home Screen.

Turn your phone sideways

The game is wide: it fills the screen when your phone is on its side.
